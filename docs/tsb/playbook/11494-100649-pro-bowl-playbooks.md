## Summary

> Theoretically, you could make any playbook you wanted for any team. With any set of designed plays, no matter its actual slot assignment. This set of plays could only be utilized by the original default ROM state, though. Attempting to change a play results in being stuck with the specific eight of that slot. > > But still. Doesn't this mean that we can have default original ROM state playbooks of whatever we want? This is such a freeing concept for me, particularly with making a Coach ROM, you have no idea. The regular playbooks and the pro bowl playbooks are written in different manners. The Pro Bowl is written slot by slot as a value of x00 = Play 0 to x3F = Play 63 and the Regular is written as two slots like x00 = Play 0 in Slot 1 and Play 8 in slot 2 to x70 = Play 7 in Slot 1…
