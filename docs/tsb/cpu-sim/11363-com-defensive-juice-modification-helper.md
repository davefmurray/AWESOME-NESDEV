## Summary

The original game started at week 1/preseason with: 00 01 00 00 00, and maxed out at 03 04 06 09 09. To make the game play original max juiced, you'd make every juice value be 03 04 06 09 09 instead of progressively gaining more juice as your win differential rises. If you're a [real weirdo](http://www.tecmobowl.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=5&t=12676), you could even unjuice it by making it be 00 01 00 00 00 2.) Note that this requires .NET (Vista/Win 7 come with this, otherwise you can download it here: [http://download.microsoft.com/download/ ... 5setup.exe](http://download.microsoft.com/download/0/6/1/061F001C-8752-4600-A198-53214C69B51F/dotnetfx35setup.exe))
