## Summary

I figured out how to edit the image at the top of the OF starters page to reflect a team having a 4WR set, a 2RB set, a 3 WR set, or just about any other formation you can think of. the code is broken into 3 sections, with each section containing 12 bytes, two for each offensive starters. there are 36 total bytes used, 12 for the 2 RB formation, 12 for the 4WR formation, and 12 for the 3WR formation.

## Key facts

- the hex is found on offset:
- 3078=QB
- 4060=RB1
- 4090=RB2
- 2838=WR1
- 30C8=WR2
- 28A8=TE
- 4078=RB
- 3048=WR1
- 2818=WR2
- 2808=WR3
- 30A8=WR4
